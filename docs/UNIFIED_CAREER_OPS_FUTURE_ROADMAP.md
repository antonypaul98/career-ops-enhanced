## Build order

### Phase 0 — End-to-end approved-application demo (MUST COME FIRST)
Target: start from a real, permitted job listing and finish with a verified, one-time submission, trustworthy lifecycle state, and an exact local application archive.

1. **Find / qualify job:** query supported company/ATS sources and respect their terms; validate live job URL, company, role, requisition, salary if supplied, location, employment type (C2C vs employee), remote/hybrid requirement, sponsorship claims and freshness. Capture the original JD bytes, source URL, source-time, source spans and dedup identity. Distinguish mandatory vs preferred requirements and unknowns.
2. **Gap questionnaire with four outcomes:** compare the JD to candidate-approved CV, master profile and evidence Vault. For unsupported required skills or claims, ask (a) **Yes, accurate** with actual context (where/when/how used); (b) **No**; (c) **Correct it**; or (d) **I don't know**. A yes to `skill_only` must not become employment experience, and a yes to a real contextual accomplishment must stay within the approved context. No automatic JD-to-experience conversion. Work authorization, EEO, compensation, dates, employers, metrics, qualifications and legal attestations are not guessed. Use the answers to revise match/eligibility and store only approved durable facts in the candidate's private data layer. **Confirming an experience fact is not approval to submit an application.**
3. **Truthful tailored package:** select an existing verified resume persona, reorder/emphasize only approved claims, create the exact PDF and optional other format, and compose job-specific answers and cover letter where needed. Report gaps and actual source bindings, check PDF bytes/hash and current facts independently; unsupported experience stays omitted. Do not promise 95% ATS matching or a 99% eligibility score without evidence.
4. **Exact one-job approval → automatic application:** show company, role, posting URL, all rendered form answers (especially sponsorship/work-authorization/salary), resume PDF, cover letter, attachment bytes and target. After one explicit final approval bound to this exact application, its destination, fields, file digests and observation, automatically fill/advance supported, authorized ATS forms and submit exactly once. Do **not** require another generic 'Are you sure?' unless a consequential detail changed. Any login, MFA, CAPTCHA, changed URL, stale session, unexpected field/control, unexpected permission requirement, attachment mismatch or altered submit page pauses safely and requests narrowly scoped user intervention or renewed approval. Never evade site protections or submit blanket batches. Reuse `start-application.mjs`, `submit-application-browser.mjs`, C12 content-bound approval and draft PR safety fixes. A click is not submission evidence.
5. **Verify employer receipt before Applied:** capture actual acknowledgement/receipt and attachment identity; independently verify the receipt and only then transition the canonical tracker to `Applied`. Keep **queued / attempted / submitted / confirmed** separate, including when email acknowledgments are delayed; idempotent and retry-safe, no duplicate submissions.
6. **Save immutable per-job archive locally on the user's Mac** in the explicitly configured private Career Ops root: `applications/<company>/<role>-<job-id>/` or an equivalent collision-safe path. Include company and role metadata, source and full raw JD, exact *submitted* resume PDF (not a later regenerated version), editable source where appropriate, submitted questions/answers and declarations, exact approved package manifest, hashes, approval evidence, ATS receipt/screenshots/reference, submission time, recruiter thread links, interview notes, round history and final outcome. Protect privacy/permissions; atomic writes, safe naming, encryption where appropriate, recoverable backups and deletion/export support. Never send sensitive records to public GitHub; updating Career Ops must not overwrite this archive.
7. **Recover the exact application later:** looking up a company and role must return the same submitted resume, JD, answers and receipt, with provenance and changes preserved. A future new version must not replace the submitted snapshot.

**Phase 0 acceptance matrix:** one realistic user-approved ATS sandbox or actually chosen real job where permitted, plus adversarial synthetic/browser tests: true/false/unknown evidence, eligibility and stale JDs, wrong attachment bytes under correct filename, same-name replacement, drifted DOM, MFA/CAPTCHA, changed page/session, cancellation/disconnection, duplicate retries, crash/restart, zero Submit before approval, exactly one Submit after approval, verified receipt before Applied, byte-for-byte Mac archive retrieval, tenant and secret isolation. Exact-head CI and real Mac/browser acceptance are separately required. Preserve ongoing C05/browser/approval PR stacks and complete their bounded reviews before declaring Phase 0 done.

### Phase 1 — Gmail, recruiter, calendar, and correspondence (after Phase 0)
- Read-only Gmail first: monitor application acknowledgements, assessments, interviewer invitations, status updates, recruiter correspondence, rejections and offers, with dedupe and exact job/thread matching. Explain uncertain matches and ask for verification. No automatic `Applied` from a guessed email.
- Draft short, truthful, application-aware recruiter replies, interview confirmations, reschedules, follow-ups and thank-you messages. Reuse exact JD/submitted CV and verified candidate facts. Recipient, thread, content, attachments and sensitive fields require **specific explicit send approval**; do not impersonate or auto-send without approval.
- Calendar integration: approved availability, timezones, conflicts, meeting links, reminders and invitation drafts; require approval to schedule/cancel/RSVP. Existing `enhanced/interview-availability.mjs` and `enhanced/interview-reply-draft.mjs` are policy/draft-only, not a live Gmail or Calendar connection.
- Offer an authorized phone/recruiter-call intake path, notes or transcripts only when legally and explicitly consented. Match recruiter identity to an application using evidence. Escalate sensitive salary/immigration answers for review.
- Save verified correspondence and meeting events to the same immutable application history; keep drafts vs sent messages and cancellations vs confirmations distinct.

### Phase 2 — Interview context, ChatGPT bridge, and integrated QuietCue
- **One-application truth:** Retrieve the precise JD, exact submitted resume, all confirmed facts, submitted answers, employer/interviewer correspondence, application timeline, approved STAR examples, assessments, prior interview questions and feedback. Never silently substitute a later CV or another job's details.
- **User-authorized ChatGPT context bridge:** provide per-job, read-only, permission-scoped export/upload/compatible connector or a local bridge that ChatGPT can access when explicitly enabled. Document exactly which ChatGPT integration is actually supported. ChatGPT does **not** automatically have access to arbitrary Mac files, local microphone, another chat's attachments or private repositories. Context must be source-cited, limited, revocable, privacy-protected and fresh. Do not send documents to an unapproved external provider. This bridge also supports interview practice using the normal ChatGPT interface.
- **Interview preparation:** official source-first company/team/recruiter and publicly available interviewer research, verified identity/role, likely technical areas, practical mock interview questions, role-focused system design/coding drill, grounded two-to-four-sentence answers, authentic STAR stories, non-fabricated follow-ups, truthful explanations of gaps and previous-round continuity. Label ambiguous interviewer identities; no invasive profiling of private individuals.
- **QuietCue inside Career Ops, not a second app:** opt-in separate microphone and Mac system-audio capture, low-latency streaming ASR partial/final text, question-end detection, answer grounding through same application vault, fast useful response display, natural style, follow-up context, interruptions, reconnection and long-session stability (90+ minute target); low-distraction desktop overlay and optional synchronized iPhone/iPad display. Maintain privacy-gated optional screen/OCR support, dormant unless requested. The iPhone camera/scanner feature stays removed. Permission prompts and OS/TCC remain user-controlled.
- **Measure speed rather than assert it:** instrument capture startup, first transcript, ASR finalization, question boundary, context lookup, prompt/LLM request, first useful answer, p50/p95 and worst-case. Compare performance against the target Parakeet workflow under controlled *actual Mac* tests; claim faster only with real evidence.
- Modes: practice, meeting assistance and live interview assistance **only where permitted**, with explicit user consent and observance of interview rules. No covert recording, hidden impersonation or bypass of employer restrictions. Maintain post-interview recap, questions, feedback and next-round preparation.

### Phase 3 — Expand coverage and learning (only after working prior phases)
- Better permission-compliant company ATS feeds, source freshness, sponsorship and employment verification: differentiate employer-confirmed, historical, third-party inference, contradictory and unknown; no automatic authorization guesses.
- Approved referral/intro requests, provenance and fees, relationship and company/team intelligence, recruiter contact history; no unsolicited automated contact.
- Assessment preparation, salary/offer comparison, negotiation drafts, follow-up cadence, reasonable application prioritization and explainable fit-score calibration.
- Career analytics across genuine job discoveries → verified fits → candidate-approved submissions with receipts → recruiter responses → interviews → offers. Never optimize for false application volume or fabricate conversion statistics.
- Unified Mac-first responsive dashboard, optional iPhone/iPad, privacy-bounded local sync and secure export; no mandatory paid keys, ChatGPT Work, Astra or other premium sessions.
